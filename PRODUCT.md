# Cafe 1 Connect: product context

Users and purpose: Single-merchant Crown Court café storefront, till, kitchen display, juror vouchers, payments, dispatch and management SaaS.

Visual baseline: Current src/styles.css uses white/near-black OKLCH surfaces, a restrained warm red primary (oklch 0.55 0.22 27), Fraunces for display and Inter for operations. Protect red warning states and existing court-specific information hierarchy.

Primary UX priorities: Different ordering modes for jurors, judges, public and staff must be clear. At the till and KDS, queue age, order numbers, preparation states, modifiers and voucher balances take priority over decoration. Large touch targets, rapid order recall, no hidden fees or invented payment confirmation.

Source code, actual runtime integrations, permissions and financial/regulatory evidence remain the source of truth, not proposed marketing mockups or demo data.
